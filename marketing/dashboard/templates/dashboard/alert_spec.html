Threshold Reference
  • 50 bps = 0.0050 absolute MTS difference (e.g. budget 25.00% vs actual 24.50%)
  • 10% YOY = (cur_rev − yoy_rev) / yoy_rev ≥ 0.10
  • 5% WOW revenue decline = (cur_rev − cmp_rev) / cmp_rev < −0.05
  • YOY window = 52-week offset (364 days) to preserve day-of-week alignment
  • MTS = Cost ÷ Revenue — lower is better
  • Alerts evaluate after pacing alignment, so partial weeks compare equal elapsed days
Precedence & Overlap
  • Alerts are not mutually exclusive — a brand can be both Overly Efficient and Missing Revenue.
  • Needs Attention and Doing Well are logically exclusive (revenue can't be both <10% and ≥110% of YOY).
  • Overly Efficient and Doing Well can coexist — a brand growing 15% YOY while 60 bps under MTS budget gets both badges.
  • Data-quality alerts (No Budget, No Revenue) suppress the alerts that depend on the missing data.
