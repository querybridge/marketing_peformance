Component Definitions

A — Spend→Click Elasticity
Log-log regression of daily spend vs clicks over the elasticity window. Higher elasticity means more clicks per incremental dollar — better scalability.
B — Budget Binding Proxy
Compares current spend to an implied campaign budget derived from last year's spend share and the vertical's monthly budget. Lower utilization = more room to grow = higher score.
C — Efficiency Stability
Weekly ROAS and CVR coefficient of variation, shrunk toward the peer group. Lower CV means more predictable performance — safer to scale.
