Instructions

Required columns: Campaign name, Date, Cost
Optional columns: Campaign ID, Campaign Type, Impressions, Clicks, Conversions, Conversion Value, Impression Share, Click Share
Upsert: Existing rows (same campaign + date) are updated; new rows are created.
Auto-create: Unknown campaigns are created with brand set to "Unknown". Campaign type is read from the CSV (or defaults to "Uncategorized"). Assign the correct brand via Match Campaigns.
Matching: Campaigns are matched by external ID first, then by name.
Brand reassignment: After upload, use Match Campaigns to reassign campaigns to the correct brand.
